# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise code, not
people.

## Standards

Expected:

- Direct, technical feedback. "This bind is not loopback-safe because X" is
  welcome and is the point of review.
- Patience with people who are new to the stack — Chrome extensions, MCP, and
  PTYs each have a learning curve.
- Accepting a "no". Not every PR fits the project, and maintainer time is
  finite.

Not acceptable:

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissing someone rather than their argument.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private, including unwelcome attention
  after being asked to stop.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues and PRs.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit messages in
this repository, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer ([@15273](https://github.com/15273))
via GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

Responses escalate as needed: a warning, removal of the offending content, or
a block from the repository. It's a side project run by one person — expect a
reply in days, not hours.
